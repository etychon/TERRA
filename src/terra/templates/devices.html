Inventory is stored in UTC; times below use your browser time zone. Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager does not expose a standard server-push feed for full device lists to external apps — TERRA refreshes on a timer and via Sync now (configurable TERRA_SDWAN_SYNC_INTERVAL_SECONDS).

Devices across SD-WAN managers

Short tap a row to open readable device details. Long-press (~0.5s) a row to enter bulk mode (selection column appears); then tap rows or use checkboxes — selection is kept when you change pages. Choose two or more devices and click Compare selected. Column order, visibility, and sort are remembered (localStorage). Account and Type are hidden by default. Everyone sees the full inventory; Account is the TERRA user who registered that SD-WAN Manager.
